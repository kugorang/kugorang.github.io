ABOUT KUGORANG

Useful tools for life,
stories worth playing.

Kugorang is an independent app and game business founded and run by Hyeonwoo Kim. Its products include Fillday, Moonjunk Workshop, and CakeTogether.

Fillday is a planner for schedules, tasks, and reflections; Moonjunk Workshop is a story game in development; CakeTogether is an app concept for preparing celebrations together. Hyeonwoo leads product planning, design, and development.
