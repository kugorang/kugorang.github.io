CakeTogether

Prepare a gift together. Keep the wishes.

An app concept that brings birthday gift pledges and wishes together in one place. Money and gifts would be exchanged outside the app.

This is a service concept, not an available app. Its name, features and release schedule have not been finalized.

a little piece of kindness.

Record a pledge, without transferring money.

The idea is to record “I plan to contribute this much.” Collecting, holding, transferring or settling gift money, purchasing gifts and arranging delivery are outside the service scope.

The experience we are exploring.

  1. 01

    Prepare together

    The proposed flow starts with a gift, a target and a deadline, followed by a shared link to invite friends.

  2. 02

    Record pledges and wishes

    Friends could record, edit or cancel their own pledge before the deadline, or simply leave a birthday wish without an amount.

  3. 03

    Keep the memories

    Even if the target is not reached, a celebration card could keep the wishes whose authors agreed to share them.

Sharing would be a personal choice.

The proposed design keeps individual amounts hidden from other participants and asks separately for consent to share names and messages on a card. This would not guarantee complete anonymity.

The concept uses Flutter. Features and the order of web and mobile releases are still being considered.
